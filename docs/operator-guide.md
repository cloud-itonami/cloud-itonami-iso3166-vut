# Operator guide — VUT

Portal: `ctb.gov.vu/en/tenders/actual-tenders` (Central Tenders Board
actual-tenders listing) for public procurement; the Board's own
homepage states procurement above VT 10,000,000 requires the Board's
own tender process (Government Contracts and Tenders Act [CAP. 245]
s.3(2)/s.11A(1)(a)), while a Director-General may enter a contract
below that threshold directly (s.3(4)).

Entity: Vanuatu Financial Services Commission (VFSC) business/company
registration (Companies Act No. 25 of 2012) plus a Business Licence
Certificate (Business Licence Act [CAP 249] 2006) plus a Department of
Customs and Inland Revenue tax record.

Foreign-owned engagements seeking a Foreign Investment Approval
Certificate (FIAC, Vanuatu Investment Promotion Authority / Foreign
Investment Act 2019) must obtain that certificate before the engagement
proceeds -- this repo's catalog cites VIPA/FIAC in the evidence
checklist but, honestly, does not independently model a sectoral
eligibility gate (no Reserved/Restricted-List text could be confirmed
this session; see `src/marketentry/facts.cljc`).

A Government Contract of VT 10,000,000 or above additionally requires a
verified Tax Administration Act No. 37 of 2018 s.51 tax clearance
certificate before submission (Government Contracts and Tenders Act
[CAP. 245] s.14(2)(e)) -- the governor HARD-holds an unverified record.

Human-gated filing only.
