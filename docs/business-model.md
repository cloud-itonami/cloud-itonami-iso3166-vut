# Business model — VUT

Independent public-sector market-entry compliance for the Republic of
Vanuatu.

- Public procurement is administered by the Central Tenders Board
  (CTB), Ministry of Finance and Economic Management (MFEM) --
  Government Contracts and Tenders Act [CAP. 245], own s.3(2)/(4): a
  Government Contract of VT 10,000,000 or above requires a minister
  and the Board's own procurement-procedure involvement (s.11A(1)(a));
  below VT 10,000,000, a Director-General (or authorised delegate) may
  enter the contract directly. Contracts exceeding VT 100,000,000
  additionally require a written submission to the Council of
  Ministers (s.3(3)(g)).
- Vanuatu Financial Services Commission (VFSC), through its online
  Registry (registry.vfsc.vu) -- company/business registration
  (Companies Act No. 25 of 2012, Business Names Act [CAP 211]) **and**,
  confirmed as a genuinely DUAL role on the SAME platform, international/
  offshore company registration (International Companies Act
  [CAP. 222], Company and Trust Services Providers Act No.8 of 2010,
  Offshore Limited Partnership Act No.39 of 2009, Mutual Funds Act
  No.38 of 2005, Foundation Act No. 38 of 2009, Unit Trusts Act No. 36
  of 2005) -- ONE body, not two separate registrars.
- Department of Customs and Inland Revenue -- Value Added Tax (VAT,
  15% since 2018, up from 12.5% under the original VAT Act No. 12 of
  1998; now governed by the Value Added Tax Act [Cap 247] and Tax
  Administration Act No. 37 of 2018), Business Licence Act [CAP 249]
  2006, and the Tax Administration Act's own s.51 tax clearance
  certificate. No income tax item was found on the Department's own
  current Taxes menu (Rent Tax, VAT, Turnover Tax only) -- an
  absence-of-finding, not proof of certainty, reported honestly rather
  than assumed from Vanuatu's historical no-income-tax reputation.
- Vanuatu Investment Promotion Authority (VIPA) -- Foreign Investment
  Act, 2019 (Act No. 25 of 2019), Foreign Investment Approval
  Certificate (FIAC). This iteration could not independently confirm
  the Act's own primary text or a Reserved/Restricted-List sectoral
  gate this session (no standalone VIPA website was reachable, and
  PacLII's 2006 consolidated edition naturally predates a 2019 Act) --
  FIAC is cited in the required-evidence checklist but is NOT modelled
  as a governor ground-truth recompute (unlike sibling jurisdictions'
  own confirmed reserved-sector gates).
- Government Contracts and Tenders Act [CAP. 245] flagship gate --
  independently recomputes whether an engagement's own declared
  contract value actually clears the VT 10,000,000 s.3(4) low-value-
  procurement threshold it claims, bars a filing that misdeclares its
  own procurement track to evade Central Tenders Board scrutiny, and
  separately (s.14(2)(e)) requires a verified Tax Administration Act
  No. 37 of 2018 s.51 tax clearance certificate for any contract at or
  above that same VT 10,000,000 threshold.
